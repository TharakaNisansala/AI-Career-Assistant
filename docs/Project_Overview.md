# AI Career Assistant Platform

## Introduction

AI Career Assistant is an AI-powered platform designed to help job seekers improve their resumes, evaluate ATS compatibility, match resumes with job descriptions, and receive personalized career guidance.

The system uses Large Language Models (LLMs) and AI-based analysis techniques to understand resume content, identify strengths and weaknesses, and provide recommendations to improve users' chances of getting shortlisted.



## Problem Statement

Many job seekers struggle to create ATS-friendly resumes and understand whether their skills match specific job requirements.

Most candidates do not know:
- Why their resume is rejected
- Which skills they are missing
- How to optimize their resume for a specific job
- How to prepare for interviews

This system aims to solve these problems by providing AI-powered resume analysis and career guidance.



## Objectives

- Analyze resumes automatically using AI
- Generate ATS compatibility scores
- Identify technical and soft skills
- Compare resumes with job descriptions
- Provide improvement recommendations
- Generate interview preparation questions
- Suggest personalized learning paths



## Target Users

- University Students
- Fresh Graduates
- Job Seekers
- Career Changers
- Junior Software Engineers



## Project Scope

The system will allow users to upload their resumes, analyze them using AI, compare them with job descriptions, and receive personalized career improvement suggestions.



## Expected Outcome

A complete AI-powered career assistant platform that helps users optimize their resumes and improve their career opportunities.
